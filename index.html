Before you visit

Good questions
to ask.

How do I book a test drive?

Use the form above to share your contact details, preferred model and date. The showroom team will confirm availability directly.

What is included in the displayed price?

The displayed figure is an ex-showroom estimate. RTO, insurance and applicable charges can affect your on-road price. Ask for an itemized written quotation.

Can you help me plan home charging?

Yes. Share your parking and electrical setup with the team so they can discuss current installation requirements and available options. Final feasibility depends on your property and location.

Where can I confirm warranty and service terms?

Request the current official warranty and service documents for the exact model and variant you plan to buy. Coverage can vary by terms.

Are finance and EMI figures guaranteed?

No. The calculator is an estimate. Lender rates, fees, eligibility and final loan terms determine the actual repayment.
